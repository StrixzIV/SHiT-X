# Past Stupid Hackathon Ideas — Inspiration for SHiT X

Reference list of the kind of projects these hackathons produce. Use for tone and scope, not as a checklist. Thai editions have few public project lists, so most examples below come from the original NYC event.

## Core premise

"Stupid Shit No One Needs and Terrible Ideas" — build something pointless on purpose, in a short window, and show it off. Fun and learning over polish or business value. Projects often follow a theme or category.

## Original NYC Stupid Hackathon (Feb 6, 2016, NYU)

Source: [Killscreen recap](https://www.killscreen.com/stupid-hackathon-celebrates-the-creativity-of-useless-tech/)

- **NonAd Block**: a Chrome extension that hides everything on a page except the ads.
- **Unfriend the Poors**: a service that removes "pesky" lower-income acquaintances from your Facebook friends list (satire of social-media classism).
- **Glass Menagerie (unseen)**: a play adaptation by someone who says they never saw or read the original.
- **Fireplace VR**: a VR experience of watching a fireplace on a TV.
- **Shakie**: a camera app that only takes a photo if you shake the phone vigorously.
- **Soylent Dick**: a novelty object that squirts out Soylent.

Other NYC-hackathon projects mentioned by a secondary source: a 3D printer that prints in Velveeta.

## Patterns across editions

- Satire of tech culture, startups, and social media.
- Mock-official or parody systems (the `IMPLEMENTATIONS.md` brief for HydroCharge Sentinel, a fake university portal, follows this pattern).
- Novelty interfaces and gimmicks: fake VR, shake-to-capture, phone-to-phone acoustic networking.
- Demo day is interactive and absurd, not a judged pitch.
- Themes are announced per edition (e.g., "Brain Rot" for 2025).
